# Field Technician Quick-Start Guide

Welcome to the **Maintenance Management** Mobile Portal. This guide outlines your day-to-day operations on the field.

## 1. Accessing the Mobile Portal
* Log in using your assigned credentials at `https://erp.elmrkz.cloud`.
* Navigate to the **Technician Dashboard** workspace to view assigned orders, active trips, and quick actions.

## 2. Shift Hours & Order Dispatch
* Your shifts are configured in your **Field Technician** profile (e.g., 08:00 to 17:00).
* Orders created outside your shift hours are automatically queued and dispatched to you when your shift starts.

## 3. Starting a Trip & GPS Navigation
* Tap **Start Trip** on any assigned service order.
* The system automatically captures your current GPS coordinates, logs a timestamped audit entry, and enables map navigation to the client site.
* Customers can track your live ETA and status via their secure tracking link.

## 4. Requesting Spare Parts
* If you need consumable parts from the warehouse during a service, tap **Request Spare Parts**.
* Select the required items and quantities; this instantly generates a linked **Material Request** in ERPNext for warehouse approval.

## 5. Completing Service & Proof of Service
* Upon completing the maintenance task:
  1. Record technician notes and observations.
  2. Request a digital signature from the customer on your mobile screen.
  3. Collect customer satisfaction rating (1-5 stars) and feedback.
* Tap **Complete Order**. This automatically generates the **Sales Invoice** (including standard service fees and parts) and **Stock Entry** for inventory deduction.
